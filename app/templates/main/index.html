About the System

Understanding Cognitive Health Through Interaction

The Cognitive Performance Monitoring System tracks subtle changes in how young adults interact with digital tasks — measuring typing patterns, reaction speeds, concentration levels, and memory performance over time.

By collecting temporal interaction data during routine assessments, researchers and clinicians can identify early signs of cognitive change before they become clinically apparent.
